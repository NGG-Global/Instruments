1. Overview

EVionics is an Android flight-instrument utility that presents attitude, heading, altitude, vertical speed and ground speed using the sensors and GNSS receiver built into your Android device. All sensor and GNSS processing is performed directly on your device. The application does not include the Android INTERNET permission and therefore has no ability to transmit data over the network.

2. Location and GNSS data

EVionics requests location permission because several instruments depend on the device's GNSS (satellite navigation) receiver:

  • ground speed
  • GNSS track over ground
  • GNSS altitude, including on-device conversion to mean sea level where the Android version supports it
  • GNSS accuracy information and satellite status shown in diagnostics
  • magnetic declination for true-heading display, computed from your position using Android's built-in geomagnetic model

Location is used in the foreground, while the application is in use. EVionics does not request Android's background-location permission and does not receive location updates when the app is not running in the foreground.

The application itself does not transmit location or GNSS information to any EVionics- or developer-operated server. It has no network access of its own.

3. Device sensor data

EVionics reads the device's motion and environment sensors to drive the instruments:

  • rotation-vector orientation sensors (the Android operating system fuses these from the accelerometer, gyroscope and magnetometer) — used for attitude and heading
  • the barometric pressure sensor, where present — used for altitude and vertical speed

The app also checks which sensors exist on your device (accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, rotation vectors, barometer) in order to select data sources and to show a hardware inventory in diagnostics. Sensor readings are processed on the device and are not transmitted anywhere by the application.

4. Diagnostic recordings

EVionics includes an optional diagnostic recording feature, started and stopped manually from the diagnostics screen. When recording is active, raw sensor samples and GNSS samples — including position, altitude, speed, bearing and accuracy values with timestamps — are written to a file in the application's private storage on your device (in the app-private recordings folder). Recordings exist so that instrument filtering can be replayed and verified deterministically inside the app. They are never uploaded by EVionics.

EVionics itself does not upload diagnostic recordings to developer-operated servers. Depending on your Android device and backup settings, locally stored application data — including settings and diagnostic recordings — may be included in backups managed by the Android operating system or your platform provider (for example, device cloud backup). Those backups are governed by your device's and platform provider's settings and policies, not by EVionics.

5. Settings and calibration data

Preferences such as the SET LEVEL attitude calibration, mounting orientation, QNH altimeter setting and heading-reference choice are stored locally on your device in the application's private storage. They are not transmitted by the application.

6. Internet access and third-party services

  • The Android application does not request the INTERNET permission.
  • There is no developer-operated backend used by the flight-instrument functionality.
  • The application contains no advertising SDKs.
  • The application contains no analytics SDKs.
  • No account is required or supported.

7. Data sharing and sale

  • EVionics does not sell user data.
  • EVionics does not share flight, sensor or location information with advertisers.
  • The developer does not receive your flight data through any EVionics backend — none exists.

This describes the behavior of the EVionics application and its developer. Android system services on your device (such as the operating system's location services or platform backup) operate under your device settings and your platform provider's policies.

8. Data retention and deletion

All EVionics data lives on your device. You can remove it at any time:

  • Android Settings → Apps → EVionics → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data removes all settings, calibration and diagnostic recordings.
  • Uninstalling the application removes its private storage.
  • In the app, the SET LEVEL attitude calibration can be cleared from the settings screen.

The current version does not provide in-app deletion of individual diagnostic recording files; use the Android storage controls above to remove them.

9. Permissions

EVionics requests precise location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) and its Android-required companion, approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION). Precise location is needed because GNSS-derived speed, track and altitude are only meaningful at full accuracy.

If you deny location permission, the application continues to function: attitude, heading and barometric altitude remain available from the device's sensors, while GNSS-dependent values — ground speed, track, GNSS altitude and true-heading declination — are shown as unavailable or degraded rather than fabricated.

10. Children's privacy

EVionics is a specialized aviation utility and is not designed or directed toward children.

11. Security

EVionics follows a local-processing model: data is processed and stored in the application's private storage area, which Android isolates from other applications, and the app has no network capability of its own. No system can guarantee absolute security; the physical security of the device and your Android security settings (such as screen lock and device encryption) remain important.
