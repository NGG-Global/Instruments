EVIONICS

Offline Flight Instruments for Android

Turn your Android phone or tablet into a sensor-powered supplemental flight instrument panel. EVionics uses your device's onboard sensors and GNSS to provide real-time attitude, heading, altitude, vertical speed and ground speed.

Privacy Policy

For supplemental and experimental use only. EVionics is not a certified flight instrument and is not a substitute for approved aircraft instrumentation or navigation equipment.

PANEL

Five primary instruments

A native panel drawn entirely on-device, in the visual language of classic aircraft instruments — dark faces, engraved scales and analog needles.

ATT

Attitude

Pitch and roll derived from the best attitude sensors available on the device, with SET LEVEL calibration.

HDG

Heading

Magnetic or true heading, with GNSS track treated separately — never relabeled as heading.

ALT

Altimeter

Barometric altitude with adjustable QNH where a pressure sensor is available, with GNSS fallback where supported.

VSI

Vertical Speed

Filtered climb and descent indication using barometric data where available, with a degraded GNSS fallback.

GS

Ground Speed

GNSS-derived ground speed in knots. No fabricated movement when a valid fix is unavailable.

SYSTEMS

Built like an instrument, not an app

Every value on the panel carries an explicit data-quality state. Unavailable data is flagged — never silently shown as zero.

Sensor-aware fallbacks
Each instrument selects the best available sensor and degrades gracefully when hardware is missing, with the consequence clearly reported.
Data-quality indicators
Per-instrument quality states (good / degraded / poor / unavailable) on the panel and in diagnostics.
SET LEVEL & mounting calibration
Quaternion-based level reference plus a mounting-orientation setting, so any rigid mount works.
Magnetic / true heading
True heading applies local magnetic declination from the on-device geomagnetic model.
Adjustable QNH
Altimeter setting in hectopascals with a one-tap reset to standard pressure 1013.25 hPa.
Day / night display
Red night-vision mode, brightness steps and a panel lock against accidental touches.
Diagnostics
Live GNSS accuracy, satellite counts, sensor inventory and per-channel data quality.
Local recording & replay
Optional on-device recording of raw sensor and GNSS samples for deterministic diagnostic replay. Never uploaded.
Portrait & landscape
The panel reflows between a portrait stack and a landscape layout with a side control rail.
Tap to enlarge
Any instrument opens full-screen for at-a-glance legibility on small displays.

PRIVACY BY ARCHITECTURE

Built to work offline

NO INTERNET PERMISSION NO ACCOUNT NO ADS · NO ANALYTICS SDK ON-DEVICE PROCESSING

All flight calculations — attitude, heading, altitude, vertical speed and ground speed — are performed locally on your device using its sensors and GNSS receiver. The Android application does not request Android's INTERNET permission, so it has no ability to transmit data over the network.

There is no account, no developer-operated backend, no advertising and no analytics SDK. Settings, calibration and optional diagnostic recordings stay in the app's local storage on your device. Details are in the privacy policy.

SAFETY NOTICE

Know what this is — and what it is not

For supplemental and experimental use only. EVionics is not a certified flight instrument and is not a substitute for approved aircraft instrumentation or navigation equipment. Consumer device sensors are affected by vibration, temperature, mounting and magnetic interference. Always fly by your aircraft's approved instruments and applicable regulations.
